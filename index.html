Hwa Shang is a senior SAP consultant and enterprise integration architect with deep expertise in SAP PI/PO, HANA Cloud Platform Integration (CPI), ABAP, and Java. Over a 27-year career, he has led and delivered complex, mission-critical integration projects for blue-chip clients across Southeast Asia, Europe, and the Americas.

His track record spans full project lifecycles — from solution architecture and prototyping through to go-live and production support. He brings rare depth across both functional process knowledge (PP, MM, SD, FICO, HR, WM, PS) and technical implementation (ABAP, Java, XSLT, Groovy, OData, Web Services, EDI).

Currently AWS-certified and delivering cloud migration engagements, Hwa Shang combines battle-tested enterprise experience with fluency in modern SAP cloud technologies — including SAP RISE, S/4HANA Cloud, and BPC 2021.
